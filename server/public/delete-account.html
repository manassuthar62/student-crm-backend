Account Deletion Request

We are sorry to see you go. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from Tech Flow Education CRM, please follow the steps below:

How to delete your account:

  • Option 1: Log in to the app, go to Settings, and click on "Delete Account".
  • Option 2: Send an email to our support team at support@techfloweducation.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and your registered mobile number/email.

Data that will be deleted:

Upon your request, we will permanently delete:

  • Your personal profile information.
  • Your login credentials.
  • Associated records (where applicable and as per regulatory requirements).

Note: It may take up to 7-10 business days to process your request.
